DESCRIPTION (everything between the two ==== lines; Etsy keeps the line breaks)

Lift it, spin it, count it.

The Rev Spinner is a fingers-only disc cut from a bowling ball's surface, with your middle and ring hole sizes and your bridge. Swing it on a short pendulum, lift with the fingers at the bottom, and let it go onto the carpet. It spins in place, and the bars engraved on the base flicker so you can count how long. More lift at the release, longer spin. It gives you feedback. It doesn't measure anything.

No thumb hole, so it works for thumb-in, thumbless and two-handed bowlers. About 100 g in PETG, light enough for a hundred carpet releases in a sitting.

To order, choose your hand and put your middle and ring hole sizes in the personalization box, with the bridge if your sheet lists it. If it doesn't, I use half an inch.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

I bowl two-handed. Does it work for me? Yes. No thumb hole, and your sheet has the two finger sizes and the bridge.

What does the count mean? Nothing on its own. It's for comparing one release with the next. Same count ten times running is the goal, not the biggest number.

What's the bridge? The distance between the two finger holes. It's on most sheets. If yours doesn't list it, I use half an inch.
