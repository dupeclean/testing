About

RollRead is one bowler with one printer.

Every part on this site is printed to order from the drill sheet you send, checked against that sheet, and sanded by hand before it goes in the box. The first Rev Ball came off the printer from my own sheet, and I roll it on my own carpet.

If something's wrong with yours, message me through the shop you ordered from. I answer every message myself.

RollRead parts are practice tools for off the lane. Roll it, never throw it. They are not bowling balls, and RollRead isn't connected to any ball maker or bowling body.
