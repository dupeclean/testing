Two drills, one ball

The yo-yo roll. Hand behind the ball, fingers lifting straight up through it, no turn. It runs straight, long and lively down the tape. You read this one off the path: short means you dumped it, a hop means the thumb hung, a curve means you turned it.

The proper release. Thumb out first, fingers lift and the hand turns through. Now the ball rolls and curves off the line. A phone on the floor behind you shows the ring as an oval at the moment of release, and the card in the box has a scale that turns that oval into your axis rotation. Same oval, same curve, same target, ten in a row. Then add speed.

It's a feedback tool, not a measuring instrument. It makes no promise about your rev rate. It shows you which release you did, ten times a night, without a lane.

How ordering works

  1. Get your drill sheet. The shop that drilled your ball has it on file; a photo of it is fine.
  2. Build it first if you like: type the sheet into the configurator, see your grip and your bullseye on the ball, and export the fit sheet. No account, nothing stored.
  3. Order on Etsy or TikTok Shop and paste your sheet, or the exported fit sheet, into the personalization box.
  4. I check the sheet, print your part, smooth the bores by hand and ship it. A ball takes a printer day, so allow 5 to 10 business days.
