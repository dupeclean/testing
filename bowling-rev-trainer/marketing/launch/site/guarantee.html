Guarantee and shipping

Everything is printed after you order it, from your sheet. That changes what a return means, so here is exactly what I stand behind.

The 30-day fit guarantee

Your fit sheet is stored with your order and every part is checked against it before it ships. If the part you receive doesn't match the sheet on your order, or a ball doesn't roll true, tell me within 30 days of delivery and I reprint it free, shipping included. You keep the first one.

"Match" means the hole sizes, spans, pitches, hand and thumb style on the part are the ones on your sheet. Bowler's tape finishes the fit, exactly as on a ball. A thumb that wants a layer or two of tape is a normal fit, and that's why tape is in the box.

If the sheet was entered wrong

If the part matches your sheet but the sheet doesn't match your ball, the part did what it was told. I'll still help: send the corrected numbers within 30 days and I reprint at a reduced price, shipping included. $25 for a puck, $15 for a spinner, and half price for a ball. The Fit Kit exists to make this rare.

Returns

  • The Rev Ball, Rev Puck and Rev Spinner are made to your measurements and can't be resold, so they aren't returnable for a refund. The guarantee above is how I make them right.
  • The Fit Kit is returnable unused within 30 days for a refund of $24 less shipping. If you use it and order a ball, the $24 comes off the ball.
  • Damaged in transit: photograph the box and the part and email me within 7 days of delivery. I reprint and reship free.

What the guarantee doesn't cover

  • Parts you or someone else printed from files
  • Wear from use, including bores opened up by sanding or by pulling tape in and out
  • Damage from throwing, dropping onto hard floors, heat, or use outside the drills on the card
  • Changes to your fit after the order was placed. A reprint from a new sheet is available at the prices above.

Shipping

I ship within the United States for now. The shipping cost is shown at checkout on Etsy or TikTok Shop before you pay, and tracking comes from the shop when the part goes out. You can change your sheet or your address until printing starts: message me through the shop or email support@rollread.com with your order number. Once printing starts the sheet is locked, because the part is already being made from it.

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, or the box arrives damaged, email me with your order number and I reprint and reship free.

Your fit sheet

I keep your fit sheet with your order so I can reprint it and so a second part matches the first. It isn't shared with anyone. Email me if you want it deleted.
