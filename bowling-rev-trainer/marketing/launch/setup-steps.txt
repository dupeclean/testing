Why this split: Etsy's personalization box takes the drill sheet and its processing time is a normal
setting. TikTok Shop's Made-to-Order selling type (3 to 14 business days of handling) is what makes a
printed-to-order ball possible there; without it a listing must dispatch in 1 to 2 business days. The site
is the demo and the brand home; it sends every order to one of the two shops, so there's no payment
processing, sales tax or dispute handling to run yourself. Shopify waits until the shops' orders would cover
$39 a month; the products can be made from this session's Shopify tools then.

Fees as checked 2026-10-26: Etsy $0.20 per listing, 6.5% of item plus shipping, 3% plus $0.25 processing,
a possible one-time set-up fee at opening ($15 to $29 in the guides), Offsite Ads 15% only on sales they
bring. TikTok Shop about 6% referral. On a $129 ball with $12 shipping, Etsy's cut is about $13.65.

TOMORROW, WHEN THE BALL IS OFF THE PRINTER
------------------------------------------

7. The hand checks on your own ball. Nothing public happens until every line passes:
   - Holes: thumb, middle and ring fit your hand the way the drilled ball does, with no more than a layer
     of tape. Measure the bores against the sheet and write them down.
   - Pitches: forward/reverse and lateral feel the same as on the drilled ball. If they feel backwards,
     stop: nothing ships until that is fixed.
   - Thumb depth: 70 mm is right for your thumb, or you know the right number.
   - Roll: after the support scar is sanded, the ball rolls straight on carpet from a push, no wobble.
   - Bullseye: the plugs and rings press in and stay in. In a slow-motion clip of your release the
     bullseye holds for the first frames, then wheels. It reads from 2 m away.
   - Track ring: the arcs sit flush in the channel; the ring's distances from the thumb and finger holes
     (on the job sheet) match the oil ring on your own ball; from behind it reads as an oval at release.
   - Print log: the real print time and grams from Bambu Studio, and the support weight, written down.
   - Gate: segments link, pins stand, a rolled ball knocks a pin down at 1.5 to 2 m. If it doesn't earn
     its place, it stays out of the box.
8. Photos and the clip from the lists at the end of each listing file. Your own photos, no stock images.
   Drop the ball photos over the renders in site/img/.
