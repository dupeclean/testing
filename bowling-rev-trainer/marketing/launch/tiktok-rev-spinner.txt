DESCRIPTION
====
Lift it, spin it, count it.

The Rev Spinner is a fingers-only disc cut from a bowling ball's surface, with your middle and ring hole sizes and your bridge. Swing it on a short pendulum, lift with the fingers at the bottom, and let it go onto the carpet. It spins in place, and the bars engraved on the base flicker so you can count how long. More lift at the release, longer spin. It gives you feedback. It doesn't measure anything.

No thumb hole, so it works for thumb-in, thumbless and two-handed bowlers. About 100 g in PETG, light enough for a hundred carpet releases in a sitting.
