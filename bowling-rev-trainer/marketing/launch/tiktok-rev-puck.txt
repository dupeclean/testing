DESCRIPTION
====
Your drill sheet, off the ball.

The Rev Puck is the part of your ball your hand touches, printed from your drill sheet: thumb, middle and ring hole sizes in 1/64-inch steps, both spans, forward or reverse and lateral pitch on every hole, right or left hand, through or blind thumb. The holes are aimed at the ball's center the way a zero-pitch hole is drilled, with your pitches on top, so your thumb and fingers oppose each other the way they do on your ball.

It's about 300 g in PETG. Light enough to hold for a long time, heavy enough that your fingertips have to work. A pocket in the base takes coins or washers when you want more weight. A tunnel through the side takes the paracord wrist cord that comes with it, so you can swing it without it going anywhere.

Four drills on the card: fingertip carries at your desk, thumb pops on the cord, slow swings to feel where the thumb leaves, and cupped-wrist holds. Ten to fifteen minutes a day. It's for the thumb leaving first and the fingers staying, in your own holes, without a fifteen-pound ball in the room.
