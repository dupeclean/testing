TIKTOK SHOP: OPENING THE SHOP AND LISTING THE THREE PRODUCTS
============================================================

Written 2026-10-26. The seller account is yours to open (ID or SSN, a US bank account and a US phone number
go into it, and I don't touch those). The three listing files in this folder give every product field.
Facts below come from seller guides and an integration guide, not from TikTok's own help pages, so when
Seller Center shows something different, Seller Center wins.

THE ONE SETTING THAT MAKES THIS WORK: MADE-TO-ORDER
---------------------------------------------------

A standard TikTok Shop listing must dispatch within 1 to 2 business days. A ball takes a printer day plus
finishing, so a standard listing would be late on day one. TikTok Shop US has a selling type called
Made-to-Order with a handling time of 3 to 14 business days, and the shipping deadline extends to match.
It is set per product (or per variant) when you add the product. If it shows greyed out for the category,
the category doesn't allow it: pick the nearest sporting-goods category that does, and if none does, publish
only what you can ship in two days (nothing, until there's stock).

Handling times to set: Rev Ball 10 business days, Rev Puck 5, Rev Spinner 3.

OPENING THE SHOP
----------------

1. seller-us.tiktok.com, Sign up, Individual seller (or your LLC later). Government ID, SSN, US bank
   account, US phone and address. Verification can take a day or two.
2. Shop name: RollRead. Link the shop to the @rollread TikTok account so videos can tag products.
3. Shipping: Seller Center, Shipping, choose shipped-by-seller for now (you print labels through TikTok or
   your own), one shipping template with your ZIP.
4. Return policy: the default accepts returns within 30 days. Customized goods are the usual exception;
   look for it in the policy settings and turn it on for these listings if it's there.
5. Fees: about 6% referral on most categories, taken from each order. Check the fee page once.

ADDING A PRODUCT (THE SAME STEPS FOR ALL THREE FILES)
-----------------------------------------------------

1. Products, Manage products, Add new product.
2. Product name, category and images from the file. The first image on a plain background.
3. Description from the file. No addresses, no email, no "find me on Etsy": TikTok forbids pointing buyers
   off the platform in a listing, and that includes the site. The site address lives in the account bio.
4. Variations from the file (hand, right or left, for the puck and spinner).
5. Price, quantity, SKU from the file.
6. Package weight and size from the file. They set the shipping charge, so weigh a packed one.
7. Selling type: Made-to-Order, handling time from the file.
8. Personalization: if the listing form has an "Enable personalization" toggle, turn it on and paste the
   instruction from the file. If it doesn't, the description already says to message the sheet after
   ordering, and you message every buyer within a day of the order.
9. Save as draft. Publish after the printed ball has passed its hand checks and the photos exist.

AFTER PUBLISHING
----------------

- The first video tags the Rev Ball: the roll test, 10 to 15 seconds, phone on the floor behind you.
- Orders go into the RollRead app as RR- numbers with the TikTok order number in the notes.
- Watch the ship-by date on every order. An MTO order still has a deadline; it's just longer.

WHAT I COULD NOT CONFIRM
------------------------

- Whether personalization text input is available on every seller's listing form. An integration guide
  describes the toggle; Seller Center is the authority.
- The exact referral rate for the sporting-goods category.
- Whether TikTok lets you exclude customized goods from returns. Check the policy settings.
