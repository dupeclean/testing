DESCRIPTION (everything between the two ==== lines; Etsy keeps the line breaks)
====
Find your size before you print your fit.

The Fit Kit is a flat sizer plate I print around the thumb and finger hole sizes you expect, in 1/64-inch steps, with the size engraved next to each bore. It comes with a roll of bowler's tape and a fitting card that explains what a good fit feels like: snug all round at the first joint, no squeeze needed to hang on.

Why it exists: printed holes come out a few tenths of a millimeter smaller than drawn. The plate comes off the same printer, in the same material, as your Rev Ball, so the bore that fits your thumb on the plate is the bore I print in your ball. The $24 comes off a Rev Ball with the code in the box.

To order, put your expected thumb, middle and ring sizes from your drill sheet in the personalization box, using the template below. No sheet? Write "unknown" and tell me whether your hand is small, medium or large, and I'll center the plate on common sizes.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Do I need a drill sheet? It helps. The plate is centered on the sizes you give me. Without one, tell me small, medium or large and I'll center it on common sizes.

How do I use it? Put your thumb in each bore in turn. The right one is snug all round at the first joint with no squeeze needed to hang on. Same for the middle and ring fingers. Write the three sizes down. Then build any fit back with the tape, exactly like on a ball.

How does the $24 credit work? A code in the box, good for $24 off a Rev Ball in this shop.

What's it made of? PETG, printed flat with no supports. I sand the bores lightly.

Is this a bowling ball? No. It's a flat fitting plate for off the lane.
