### BLOCK: What you practice

**Coaches agree on where revs come from. FitSlice lets you practice it with your real fit.**

Coaches agree the thumb must leave first, and a relaxed hand creates more revs. Oversized holes make you squeeze; squeezing limits hook. Those are coaching views, and they are the reason a thumb-in bowler's fit matters so much: the pro-shop trade body puts hole size at about 20% of a fit, with span and pitch at 40% each. We copy all three.

What that means in your hands:

- **A relaxed grip under load.** The Rev Puck is designed for fingertip grip holds in the exact position you bowl in, with the thumb loose and the fingers carrying the weight. At about 300 g it is a feel-and-habit tool. Add weight to the pocket when you want more load. If your thumb knuckle whitens, you are probably squeezing. Reset and go again.
- **A clean, early thumb exit.** Practice a clean, early thumb exit with the thumb-pop drill: relax the thumb, let it slide out, and let the fingers catch the weight. If the puck falls, the fingers were not carrying it. You cannot drill that safely with a fifteen-pound ball in your living room.
- **Finger lift you can count.** Release the Rev Spinner onto carpet and count how long it spins. Try to beat your count.

What FitSlice does not do: it does not teach footwork, timing or targeting, and it copies whatever fit you give it. To our knowledge, no study has measured whether any off-lane trainer changes a bowler's results on the lane, and that includes us. We tell you what it is, and your hand tells you whether it feels like your ball.

## PAGE: Help · How to read your drill sheet

**How to read your drill sheet**

Every pro shop writes its sheet a little differently, but the same numbers are always there. This page shows where they live and which configurator field each one goes into. Keep your sheet open while you read.

[IMAGE: photo of a typical handwritten pro-shop drill sheet, the hand diagram with three holes, numbers written beside each hole and along the span lines. Shop name and bowler name blurred.]

### What is on a drill sheet

Most sheets have a drawing of a hand or three circles. Around the drawing you will find:

1. **Hand.** R or L, or "Right" or "Left". Sometimes only implied by the drawing.
2. **Hole sizes.** One fraction next to each hole: thumb, middle finger, ring finger. Written in 64ths or 32nds of an inch, like 1-3/64, 55/64, 27/32. Some shops write decimals.
3. **Spans.** Two measurements, usually written along lines from the thumb hole to each finger hole. The middle span is normally a little longer than the ring span. Most shops measure "cut to cut": from the near edge of the thumb hole to the near edge of the finger hole. That is what the configurator uses.
4. **Forward or reverse pitch.** One fraction with a direction next to each hole: "3/8 fwd", "1/8 rev", "0". Fingers are usually forward. The thumb is often zero or reverse.
5. **Lateral pitch.** One fraction with "left" or "right" next to each hole, or "0". Often blank, which means zero.
6. **Grip type.** Fingertip or conventional. FitSlice is built around fingertip grips.
7. **Inserts and slugs.** If you use finger inserts, the sheet may list the drilled hole (often 31/32 or 1") and the insert's inner size separately. If you use a thumb slug or interchangeable thumb, the sheet lists the size drilled into the slug.
8. **Bridge.** The distance between the finger holes. Usually 1/4". Only some sheets list it.
9. **Date and tape.** How much tape was in the thumb at fitting. Useful to know, but not entered. You finish the fit with tape on the trainer, as on your ball.

If your shop keeps your sheet in software, ask for a printout or a photo.

### An example sheet

Below is a complete right-hand fingertip sheet as a shop might write it. The numbers are a worked example, not a template. Use yours.

```
PRO SHOP DRILL SHEET                         Hand: R        Grip: Fingertip
Bowler: ______________________               Date: ________ Thumb: Slug, through
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hole          Size          Fwd / Rev pitch       Lateral pitch
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thumb         1-3/64        1/8 reverse           1/8 right
Middle        55/64         3/8 forward           0
Ring          27/32         5/16 forward          1/8 right
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Span (cut to cut)       Middle: 4-13/32          Ring: 4-9/32
Bridge: 1/4
Tape at fitting: 1 piece white in thumb
```

### Reading it line by line

| What the sheet says | Where it is | Configurator field | Enter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand: R | Top of sheet, or the drawing | Hand | Right |
| Thumb 1-3/64 | Beside the thumb hole | Thumb size | 1 3/64 |
| Middle 55/64 | Beside the middle hole | Middle size | 55/64 |
| Ring 27/32 | Beside the ring hole | Ring size | 27/32 |
| Middle span 4-13/32 | Along the thumb-to-middle line | Middle span | 4 13/32 |
| Ring span 4-9/32 | Along the thumb-to-ring line | Ring span | 4 9/32 |
| Thumb 1/8 reverse | Beside the thumb hole | Thumb forward/reverse | -1/8 |
| Middle 3/8 forward | Beside the middle hole | Middle forward/reverse | 3/8 |
| Ring 5/16 forward | Beside the ring hole | Ring forward/reverse | 5/16 |
| Thumb 1/8 right | Beside the thumb hole | Thumb lateral | 1/8 |
| Middle 0 | Beside the middle hole | Middle lateral | 0 |
| Ring 1/8 right | Beside the ring hole | Ring lateral | 1/8 |
| Thumb: Slug, through | Top of sheet | Thumb style | Through |
| Bridge 1/4 | Bottom of sheet | Bridge | 1/4 |

The configurator accepts fractions typed any of these ways: `55/64`, `1 3/64`, `1-3/64`, `4-13/32`. It also accepts decimals and millimeters if you switch units.

### The four things people get wrong

**Forward is positive, reverse is negative.** "1/8 reverse" goes in as -1/8. "3/8 forward" goes in as 3/8. If your thumb hole in the preview leans the same way as your finger holes and your sheet says reverse, you have the sign wrong.

**Lateral pitch depends on your hand.** The configurator treats pitch toward your bowling-hand side as positive. For a right-handed bowler, "right" is positive and "left" is negative. For a left-handed bowler, "left" is positive and "right" is negative. The preview draws the hole axes, so compare them with your ball before you order.

**Inserts: enter the size your finger feels.** If you use finger inserts, enter the inner size of the insert, the size listed for your finger. The larger hole drilled to accept the insert is a different number. The trainer is built around what your fingers touch.

**Spans are cut to cut.** If your shop lists center-to-center spans, say so in the order notes and we will convert them, or ask the shop for the cut-to-cut numbers. The difference is half a thumb hole plus half a finger hole, which is far too much to ignore.

### If a number is missing

- Lateral pitch blank: enter 0.
- Bridge blank: leave the default 1/4".
- Finger pitch blank: ask the shop. Do not guess forward pitch.
- Span blank: ask the shop. Spans are about 40% of a fit, by the pro-shop trade body's rule of thumb. Do not estimate them.

### Still unsure

Take a photo of the sheet and email it to support@fitslice.com with "drill sheet" in the subject line. We will read it with you and send back the configurator values. Or hand the sheet to your pro shop and ask them to enter it on the Pro Shops page. They earn 25% on the order for doing it.

## PAGE: Privacy and your fit sheet

**Privacy and your fit sheet**

Your drill sheet describes your hand. We treat it as personal information, and this page says exactly what we do with it.

### What we collect

- **Your fit sheet:** hand, hole sizes, spans, pitches, thumb style, bridge, and any options you set in the configurator. If you type a name on the sheet, we store that too.
- **Order details:** name, email, shipping address, and the items you bought. Payment is handled by our payment processor; we never see your full card number.
- **Configurator use:** the configurator runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us until you choose to save a fit sheet, join the waitlist or place an order.

### Why we keep your fit sheet

- To print your part and check it against the sheet before it ships
- To honor the fit guarantee
- To reprint it when your fit changes, without you re-entering everything

### How long

We keep your fit sheet for three years from your last order so a reprint uses the same numbers. After that it is deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time, and we do so within 30 days of your request, except where we must keep order records for tax or accounting.

### Who sees it

- FitSlice staff who print and check your order.
- A printing partner, only if we send your order to one during busy periods, and only the fit sheet and shipping label needed to make and ship your part. Partners work under a written agreement that limits use of your data to your order.
- Your pro shop, if the shop entered the sheet for you. The shop already has your sheet; we do not send them anything new.
- Our shop platform, payment processor and email provider, in the normal course of running the store.

We do not sell fit sheets or any other customer data. We do not share your fit sheet with ball manufacturers, pro shops you did not order through, or anyone else for marketing.

### Your choices

- **See it:** your fit sheet is on every order in your account and in your confirmation email.
- **Export it:** download it as a JSON file the configurator can reload.
- **Correct it:** change it free until printing starts.
- **Delete it:** email support@fitslice.com with "delete my fit sheet" and your order email address.

### Children

FitSlice does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Youth bowlers' fit sheets should be entered by a parent or guardian.
