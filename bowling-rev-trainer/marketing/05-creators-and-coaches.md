## 1. Seeding program

### 1.1 The idea in one paragraph

Twenty people who teach, review or talk about the one-handed release get a Rev Puck and a Rev Spinner printed from their own drill sheet, free, in the first six weeks. We ask for their sheet, a shipping address and honest feedback. We do not ask for a post. The product is the kind that has to be held to be understood, and these are the hands that other bowlers trust. If the fit is right, the unboxing writes itself. If the fit is wrong, we would rather hear it from twenty experts in week four than from two hundred customers in month four.

### 1.2 Who gets a free pair

| Tier | Who | Units | What ships |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Instruction creators with large audiences who bowl one-handed, thumb-in, and teach the release on camera | 4 | Pair printed from their sheet, sizer plate, tape, fitting card, drill guide |
| B | Mid-size coach-creators who review equipment with their own hands and publish verdicts | 6 | Same |
| C | Certified local coaches with an active roster of ten or more students, in markets where we can also onboard a pro shop | 6 | Same, plus the coach program packet (Section 5) |
| D | Hosts of bowling podcasts with a regular release-and-equipment segment | 2 | Same, one per host |
| Reserve | Held for a two-handed creator (Rev Spinner only, finger sizes and pitches) and for one reprint if a seeded unit misses its sheet | 2 | As needed |

Pro-shop operators are seeded separately with a counter kit and sizer plate under the pro-shop program. They do not come out of these twenty.

### 1.3 Selection criteria

Every recipient meets all of the following:

1. Bowls one-handed with the thumb in the ball and has a current drill sheet, or bowls two-handed and has finger sizes and pitches (Rev Spinner only).
2. Has published or coached in the last 90 days. A dormant channel teaches nobody.
3. Reaches league bowlers, adult or youth. Trick-shot and entertainment channels are a different audience and are not seeded in this round.
4. Agrees in advance to the disclosure line if they ever mention the product. A person who will not disclose does not receive a unit, however large the audience.
5. Is willing to tell us where it falls short. We say this in the first email so nobody is surprised by the feedback form.

### 1.4 What we ask for

- The drill sheet: hand, thumb style (through or blind), thumb, middle and ring hole sizes, middle and ring spans, forward/reverse and lateral pitch for each hole. A photo of the pro shop's sheet is enough. We enter it, send back the 3D preview and the fit sheet as entered, and print only after they confirm it.
- A shipping address.
- Feedback within three weeks of delivery, on a ten-question form that takes five minutes: does the thumb feel like your thumb hole, do the fingers, how many layers of tape did you add and where, which drills you tried, what broke or annoyed you, what you would change, and whether you would pay $89 for it.
- Permission to use that feedback internally, unattributed, to decide the change list for the production files.
- If they publish anything with the product in frame: the disclosure line, first, spoken and written.

### 1.5 What we do not ask for

- A video, a post, a story, a mention or a review. Zero obligation is stated in writing in the first email and again in the box.
- A positive opinion. The feedback form has no star rating.
- A review on our store or on any marketplace. Seeded units have a material connection and we keep them out of the review pool (Section 6).
- Approval of a script, an edit, a title or a thumbnail. We never see it before it is published.
- Removal or softening of criticism. If a seeded creator says the thumb bore ran tight, that stays in, in their video and in any excerpt we repost (Section 8).
- Exclusivity. They may review, use or promote any other product.
- A posting timeline. If a video appears in month six, that is fine.
- A quote. If we want to quote them, we ask after they have said the words, and the layout carries `[QUOTE PLACEHOLDER — name]` until they agree.

### 1.6 What we promise them

- The part is printed from their sheet and checked bore by bore against it before it ships. The 30-day fit guarantee applies to seeded units exactly as it does to paid ones: if the part misses the sheet, we reprint free. If the sheet was entered wrong, we reprint free anyway, once, because we entered it.
- We tell them the truth about what we know. The box card says, in the same words we use on the site, that no study has measured any off-lane trainer and that we are running a pilot.
- We never post about having sent them a unit unless they post first, and we never tag them into a conversation they did not start.
- Their drill sheet is personal data. It is stored with the seed record, used only to print their parts, and deleted on request.

### 1.7 What is in the seed box

- Rev Puck printed from their sheet (PETG, four walls, 20% infill, about 300 g), bores smoothed.
- Rev Spinner from the same sheet (about 100 g).
- Sizer plate with labeled bores in 1/64" steps centered on their sizes, printed on the same printer, so they can see for themselves which bore their thumb lands in and compare it to the sheet.
- Roll of bowler's tape.
- Paracord wrist cord, pre-threaded.
- Fitting card: their fit sheet as printed, the tape-up instructions, the safety line (swing-and-hold and carpet-release drills only; never throw it; bowlers under 12 with an adult present).
- Drill guide: the six drills from the README, one page.
- A printed note from the founder, four sentences: thanks, nothing is owed, the disclosure line if they ever show it, and the email address for feedback.

Nothing in the box asks for a post.

### 1.8 Process and timeline

| Week | Step |
|---|---|
| 3 | Send the outreach emails in Section 2. One follow-up at day 10. Stop when they reply. |
| 3 to 4 | Collect sheets. Enter each one, send the preview and the fit sheet as entered, get a "yes, that is my sheet" before printing. |
| 4 to 5 | Print and ship in the order sheets were confirmed. Email tracking. |
| 5 to 8 | Feedback forms come back. Weekly review of every answer. Reprint anything that missed. |

### 1.9 Budget

Landed cost of a Rev Puck is about $27 to $30 before shipping (business plan Part 5.2). A Rev Spinner and a sizer plate add roughly $10 of filament, machine time and handling between them. Domestic shipping for a 600 to 900 g box runs about $8 to $12 in our experience. Call it $50 a box and $1,000 for twenty, plus two reprints held in reserve. That is the whole cost of the program. No one is paid to post.

### 1.10 The disclosure that travels with every seeded unit

Every seeded unit, in every context where we or the recipient mention it, carries: "FitSlice sent me this trainer, printed from my own drill sheet, free of charge." When we repost a seeded creator's content we add our own version in the caption: "This creator received a FitSlice trainer printed from their own drill sheet at no charge." If they also hold a code, we add: "They earn a commission on orders placed with their code."

## 3. Affiliate terms

These terms apply to creators, coaches and podcast hosts who hold a FitSlice code. Pro shops are paid under the pro-shop program, at 25%, and do not hold affiliate codes. FitSlice may revise these terms with 30 days' written notice; commissions already earned are paid on the terms in force when the order shipped.

### 3.1 Who can join

- A bowler, coach or bowling creator with a real audience: a channel, a roster, a podcast, a club, a league. We confirm the audience exists before issuing a code.
- Someone who has agreed to the disclosure rules in 3.6 and the claims rules in 3.7 in writing.
- Coupon, deal, cashback and discount-aggregator sites are not accepted. Neither are accounts whose only content is affiliate links.

A seeded creator may hold a code. A seeded creator is never required to.

### 3.2 Commission

- **Rate:** 15% of the sale.
- **"The sale" means** the product subtotal the buyer pays for FitSlice products on an attributed order, after any discount and after the Fit Kit credit, before shipping and tax.
- **Fit Kit then trainer.** A buyer who orders a $24 Fit Kit and later a Rev Puck pays $24 and then $65. The affiliate earns $3.60 and then $9.75, which is $13.35, the same 15% of $89.
- **Reversals.** A commission is reversed if the order is refunded, charged back or cancelled before printing. A free reprint under the fit guarantee does not reverse anything. A reduced-price reprint ($25 puck, $15 spinner, $35 pair) earns 15% of the reprint price if attributed.
- **Self-purchase.** No commission on the affiliate's own orders or on orders shipped to the affiliate's address.
- **Team orders.** A coach's team order at team price (Section 5.4) earns no commission. Team pricing and commission do not stack.

Earnings at list price:

| Product | List | Affiliate earns |
|---|---|---|
| Fit Kit | $24 | $3.60 |
| Rev Spinner | $45 | $6.75 |
| Rev Puck | $89 | $13.35 |
| Pair | $119 | $17.85 |
| Digital Files, Rev Puck | $29 | $4.35 |
| Digital Files, Pair | $39 | $5.85 |

### 3.3 Attribution

- **Code at checkout.** A buyer who enters a valid code at checkout attributes the order to that code's holder. A code works at any time; it does not expire with the cookie.
- **Cookie.** A click on an affiliate link sets a 60-day cookie. An order placed within 60 days of the last click with no code entered is attributed to the last click.
- **One commission per order.** Where more than one party could claim an order, the order goes to the first that applies: (1) a pro-shop code attached to the buyer's fit sheet, because the shop entered the fit; (2) a code entered at checkout; (3) the last affiliate click within 60 days. Nobody is paid twice on one order.
- **What the buyer gets for using the code.** Free US shipping on the order. Pairs already ship free, so on a Pair the code is attribution only. This gives the buyer a reason to type the code and gives the affiliate a reason to say it.
- **Cross-device.** A cookie cannot follow a buyer from a phone to a laptop. The code can. Affiliates are told to say the code out loud and to pin it.

### 3.4 Code format

- `FIT-` followed by four to eight uppercase letters or digits of the affiliate's choosing, for example `FIT-LANE7` or `FIT-REVCOACH`. One code per affiliate. Codes are unique and are not reassigned after a program ends.
- A code may not contain a ball company's name, a governing body's initials, a competitor's product name, the word PRO, or any word from the prohibited-claims list in 3.7.
- A code is a credential. It may be shared with an audience in any medium the affiliate controls. It may not be posted to coupon sites, placed in another creator's comment section, or sold.
- The affiliate link is `{{site_url}}/?ref=FIT-XXXX` and lands on the configurator with the code pre-filled.

### 3.5 Payout

- Commissions are earned when the attributed order ships, since parts are printed to order and an order can be cancelled before printing.
- Payment by ACH or PayPal by the 10th of the following month for orders shipped in the prior month, with a statement listing each order by order number, product and commission. No minimum payout.
- US affiliates complete a W-9 before the first payout and receive the tax form the IRS requires for the year's total. Affiliates outside the US complete the applicable W-8 form and are paid by PayPal.
- A statement is final 30 days after it is issued unless the affiliate raises a discrepancy by email in that time.

Every post, video, livestream, newsletter, lesson handout or spoken mention that includes a FitSlice product, link or code carries a clear material-connection disclosure. The rules by medium:

### 3.7 Prohibited claims

An affiliate may not say, write, show on screen or imply any of the following with a FitSlice product, link or code present:

1. Any number for rev rate, RPM, hook, entry angle, pin count or average, before, after or as a comparison. This includes the affiliate's own measured numbers.
2. That the product improves, increases, boosts, adds, fixes, cures, corrects or prevents anything, including rev rate, hook, scores, grip pressure, thumb hang or any injury or pain. The words we use are "designed to", "practice", "train" and "rehearse".
3. That the product prevents or treats bowler's thumb, tendonitis or any medical condition, or is a medical device.
4. The name, logo, product name, slogan or trade dress of any bowling ball company, in a comparison or otherwise, while FitSlice is in frame or in the text. Describing a ball by its color or its weight is fine. Comparisons are made by category only: "generic one-size practice balls", "S/M/L wrist braces", "roller trainers that need your ball".
5. That FitSlice is approved, certified, endorsed, sanctioned or recommended by USBC, PBA, PWBA, IBPSIA or any association, governing body, team or federation, or that it is used in or legal for competition.
6. "Patented", "patent pending", "proprietary technology" or any statement about patent status. The brand carries `[PATENT STATUS]` where such a line may one day go, and only we fill it.
7. That the product is a bowling ball, a mini ball, a practice ball or regulation equipment. It is a trainer, a slice, a puck, a disc, a plate.
8. "The only" or "the first" without the qualifier "to our knowledge".
9. Results from any other person, named or unnamed, including students, teammates or commenters.
10. That any result the affiliate reports is typical, or any result at all stated as a fact. The affiliate speaks only to their own experience of a drill.
11. Anything the affiliate has not personally experienced.
12. Any discount, price or offer we have not published. The only buyer-side benefit of a code is free US shipping (3.3).

The affiliate may say every green claim and every qualified yellow claim listed in the creator brief (Section 4.5), and anything true about their own experience of holding the part and doing the drills.

### 3.8 Prohibited practices

- Paid search or paid social ads for FitSlice, or bidding on "FitSlice", its misspellings or "rev trainer" with our name, without written permission for that campaign.
- Using "FitSlice" or a confusingly similar word in a domain, handle, channel name or display name.
- Posting the code or link on coupon, cashback or deal sites, in marketplace listings, or in the comments of content the affiliate does not own.
- Incentivizing reviews of FitSlice on any platform, or leaving a review of FitSlice without the disclosure line in the review text.
- Cookie stuffing, redirect chains, or any method of setting a cookie without a deliberate click.
- Buying through the affiliate's own link or code, or through a household member, to earn commission.

### 3.9 Monitoring and correction

We read every post, video and episode that uses a code or tags FitSlice, because the FTC expects an advertiser to monitor the people it pays. When something is wrong:

1. **First occurrence.** We send a notice naming the post and the problem (missing disclosure, prohibited claim, prohibited practice). The affiliate corrects the post within 48 hours. A video that cannot be re-edited gets a pinned comment and a corrected description, and the next video carries the disclosure properly.
2. **Second occurrence** of the same kind within twelve months: the code is paused for 30 days. Orders placed during the pause are not attributed to it.
3. **Third occurrence**, or any single occurrence of a rev-rate number, an injury claim or a governing-body approval claim that is not corrected within 48 hours: the agreement ends and unpaid commissions on the affected content are forfeited.

### 3.10 Term and termination

- Either party may end the agreement with 30 days' written notice. Commissions earned to the end date are paid on the next statement.
- On termination, the affiliate removes the code and link from active posts within 30 days where that is practical, and leaves the disclosure in place on anything that stays up.
- The content-rights clause in Section 8 survives termination for the license period stated there, and either party may withdraw future use as that clause describes.

## 4. Creator brief: unboxing and first-use video

This brief goes in the seed box and by email to every creator who asks what we would like to see. It is a brief. Nothing in it is a contract term, and nothing in it is required. A creator who films none of it has kept their end of the deal in full.

### 4.1 What this is

FitSlice is a slice of regulation 8.5" ball surface with your drill sheet reproduced in it: thumb, middle and ring hole sizes in 1/64" steps, both spans, forward/reverse and lateral pitch on every hole, right or left hand. The holes are aimed at the ball's center the way a zero-pitch hole is drilled, so your thumb and fingers oppose each other exactly as on your ball. The Rev Puck weighs about 300 g in PETG at 20% infill and has a weight pocket in the base and a tunnel for the wrist cord. The Rev Spinner is a fingers-only disc from the same surface, about 100 g, with engraved bars on the base for counting a spin.

Your box also holds a sizer plate printed on the same printer, with labeled bores in 1/64" steps around your sizes, a roll of bowler's tape, a wrist cord, your fit sheet as printed, and the six-drill guide.

### 4.2 Your disclosure

You received this free, printed from your own drill sheet. If you show it, say so first, on camera and in the first line of the description: "FitSlice sent me this trainer, printed from my own drill sheet, free of charge." If you hold a code, add: "I earn a commission when you order with my code." Say both again when you give the code. This is required by the FTC Endorsement Guides and by us. It is the one thing in this brief that is not optional.

### 4.3 Before you film

- Read the fitting card. Compare the fit sheet on it to your pro shop's sheet. If a number differs, email creators@fitslice.com before you film and we will reprint.
- Check the thumb first. That is what every bowler watching will want to see.
- Tape up the way you would on a ball. A layer or two in the thumb is normal fit and is why tape is in the box. Tell the viewer how many layers you used.
- Safety: this is a swing-and-hold and carpet-release tool. Never throw it. Keep the wrist cord on for every swing drill. Bowlers under 12 use it with an adult present.
- Keep ball brands out of frame or out of focus when the trainer is in the shot. Describe your ball by color or weight if you need to.
