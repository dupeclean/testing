FITSLICE 1.0.0
Drill sheet in, print-ready STL out. Everything stays on this computer.
No account, no internet connection and no other software are needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------
1. START THE APP (first time)
------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All".
   Put the folder somewhere you will keep it, for example C:\FitSlice
   (do not run the app from inside the zip).
b. Open the folder and double-click  FitSlice.exe
c. Windows may show a blue box, "Windows protected your PC". That is
   because the app is not signed with a paid publisher certificate.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do this once.
d. Tip: right-click FitSlice.exe, "Show more options", "Send to",
   "Desktop (create shortcut)".

------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAKE A CUSTOMER'S PARTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Switch the units to INCHES (top right) so you can type the sheet
   exactly as written: 55/64, 1 1/32, 4-1/2, -1/8.
b. Fill in the drill sheet: hand, the three hole sizes, both spans,
   forward/reverse and lateral pitch for each hole, bridge.
   Check the 3D preview. The blue lines are the hole directions: compare
   them with the customer's ball.
c. Click "New order". Type the customer's name (required), pick the
   product, and click "Save and export print files".
d. A folder is created for the order, for example
      Documents\FitSlice Orders\FS-0001 Jordan Lee\
   It holds the STL file(s), fit_sheet.json and job_sheet.txt.
   Open the STL in your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, Cura).
   Print settings are on the job sheet: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls,
   20% gyroid infill, flat side down, no supports.

------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RUN YOUR ORDERS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Orders" to see every order, search by name or number, change
the status (New, Printing, Shipped, Done, On hold), and reopen an order
to see its saved drill sheet again. "Export orders to spreadsheet" makes
orders.csv you can open in Excel.

Order numbers are never reused, even if you delete an order. Deleting
an order does not delete its exported files.

------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHERE EVERYTHING IS SAVED (and how to back it up)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Print files:  Documents\FitSlice Orders\
              (change it any time: File > Choose orders folder)
Order book:   %APPDATA%\FitSlice\orders.json
              (paste %APPDATA%\FitSlice into the File Explorer address bar)

BACK UP the folder %APPDATA%\FitSlice regularly (copy it to a USB stick
or a cloud folder). It holds your customers' names, contact details
and drill sheets. The app also keeps one automatic previous copy
(orders.json.bak) and restores it if the file is ever damaged.

To move to a new computer: copy that folder and your FitSlice Orders
folder, then install the app the same way.

------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GOOD TO KNOW
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Holes are ROUND. If a sheet lists oval holes, enter the round size
  that fits (the Fit Kit sizer plate helps you find it) and finish the
  fit with bowler's tape.
- Printed holes come out a few tenths of a millimeter small. The app
  adds a print allowance (0.4 mm by default). Print a sizer plate on your
  own printer to see what it needs: use product "Fit Kit", then set
  "Hole sizes come from" to the sizer plate.
- The model warns you in orange when holes would run into each other.
  The same warnings are repeated on the job sheet.
- The training parts are for swing-and-hold and carpet-release drills
  only. Never throw them.

Questions or problems: write down what you clicked and what the screen
said, and keep the job_sheet.txt for the order.
