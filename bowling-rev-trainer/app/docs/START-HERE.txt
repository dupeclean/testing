FITSLICE 1.0.0
Drill sheet in, print-ready STL out. FitSlice itself never connects to the
internet and needs no account or other software.

------------------------------------------------------------------
1. START THE APP (first time)
------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All".
   Put the folder somewhere you will keep it, for example C:\FitSlice
   (do not run the app from inside the zip).
b. Open the folder and double-click  FitSlice.exe
c. Windows may show a blue box, "Windows protected your PC". That is
   because the app is not signed with a paid publisher certificate.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do this once.
d. Tip: right-click FitSlice.exe, "Show more options", "Send to",
   "Desktop (create shortcut)".

------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAKE A CUSTOMER'S PARTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Switch the units to INCHES (top right) so you can type the sheet
   exactly as written: 55/64, 1 1/32, 4-1/2, -1/8.
   If you type a number in the wrong unit (110 while the switch is on
   inches, or 4 5/16 while it is on mm) the field turns red and the
   status line says so; nothing is saved until it is fixed.
b. Fill in the drill sheet: hand, the three hole sizes, both spans,
   forward/reverse and lateral pitch for each hole, bridge.

   WHICH HOLE IS WHICH: look at the sheet with the thumb hole at the
   bottom, the way it is drawn. For a RIGHT hand the LEFT finger hole is
   the middle finger and the RIGHT hole is the ring finger. A left hand is
   the mirror image. Put each hole's numbers on the row with the matching
   side (the rows are labelled, for example "Middle - left hole"), even if
   the sheet itself only says "left" and "right". If you are unsure,
   ask the shop which hole is which before you print.

   Pitch: forward (+) leans the inner end of a hole toward the middle of
   the grip (thumb hole toward the fingers, finger holes toward the
   thumb); reverse (-) the other way. Lateral (+) is toward the RIGHT of
   the sheet for a right hand (toward the left for a left hand).
   Check the 3D preview. The blue lines are the hole directions: compare
   them with the customer's ball.
c. Click "New order". Type the customer's name (required), pick the
   product, and click "Save and export print files".
   The drill sheet on the main screen goes with the order, including any
   numbers left over from the last customer, so check it first.
d. A folder is created for the order, for example
      Documents\FitSlice Orders\FS-0001 Jordan Lee\
   It holds the STL file(s), fit_sheet.json and job_sheet.txt.
   Open the STL in your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, Cura).
   Print settings are on the job sheet: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls,
   20% gyroid infill, flat side down, no supports.
   Export again after any change: the same folder is reused. Files with
   the same name are replaced by the new version. Files from an earlier
   export that no longer apply (for example an STL named for the old hole
   sizes) are moved to a "superseded" folder inside it, not deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RUN YOUR ORDERS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Orders" to see every order, search by name or number, and reopen
an order to see its saved drill sheet again. Changing the status (New,
Printing, Shipped, Done, On hold) or any other field only counts once you
click "Save order". If you try to leave an order with unsaved changes,
FitSlice asks first.
"Export orders to spreadsheet" makes orders.csv you can open in Excel.
Close orders.csv in Excel before exporting again, or Windows will not let
FitSlice replace it.

Order numbers are never reused, even if you delete an order. Deleting
asks you to confirm; it does not delete the order's exported files.

------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHERE EVERYTHING IS SAVED (and how to back it up)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Print files:  Documents\FitSlice Orders\
              (change it any time: File > Choose orders folder)
Order book:   %APPDATA%\FitSlice\orders.json
              (paste %APPDATA%\FitSlice into the File Explorer address bar)

FitSlice never sends any of this anywhere. But if Windows backs up your
Documents folder to OneDrive (or another sync service), the print files
and orders.csv, which lists your customers' names, emails, phones and
addresses, are backed up with it. Choose another orders folder if you do
not want that.

BACK UP the folder %APPDATA%\FitSlice regularly (copy it to a USB stick
or a cloud folder you trust). It holds your customers' names, contact
details and drill sheets. The app also keeps one automatic previous copy
(orders.json.bak) and restores it if the file is ever damaged; if that
happens it tells you, and order numbers already used are not reused.

To move to a new computer: close FitSlice, copy ONLY orders.json (and
orders.json.bak and orders.json.seq if present) from %APPDATA%\FitSlice to
the same folder on the new computer, copy your FitSlice Orders folder, and
install the app the same way. Do not copy settings.json: it remembers a
folder path from the old computer.

------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GOOD TO KNOW
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Holes are ROUND. If a sheet lists oval holes, enter the round size
  that fits (the Fit Kit sizer plate helps you find it) and finish the
  fit with bowler's tape.
- Printed holes come out a few tenths of a millimeter small. The app
  adds a print allowance (0.4 mm by default). To find out what your own
  printer needs: make a "Fit Kit" order (it exports a sizer plate centred
  on the sizes entered), or click the "Sizer" button and then "Download
  STL". Print the plate and find the bore that fits the finger snugly.
  Each bore has its size engraved in millimetres. Switch the units to mm,
  set "Hole sizes come from" to "The printed sizer plate" (this sets the
  allowance to 0, because the plate already includes your printer), and
  type the engraved numbers as the hole sizes for that customer.
- The REV BALL is the release trainer: a full 200 mm ball with the
  customer's grip. Choose the part "Ball" (or product "Rev Ball"). Slice
  it with 3 walls and 5% infill, grip holes facing up, tree supports
  under the lower half; snap the supports off and sand the scar smooth.
  About 475 g and 10 to 14 hours. It prints in TWO colours with no
  painting: after slicing, right-click the layer slider in Bambu Studio
  at each height listed on the job sheet and choose "Add color change";
  the printer pauses there so you can swap the filament (with an AMS it
  swaps by itself). Start in the base colour. The coloured top cap and
  the band round the middle make the spin easy to see. To use it: kneel, swing low, let it go along a strip of tape
  on the floor with a couch or wall 1.5 to 2 m away. Rolls and curves off
  the line = thumb out first, fingers lifted. Straight and short = dumped.
  Pops up = thumb hung up. Spins in place = hand on top. The thumb hole
  is blind (70 mm deep by default) like a real ball.
- The Rev Spinner uses only the two finger hole sizes and the bridge;
  its holes are drilled straight at the ball's center, so spans and
  pitches apply to the Rev Puck only.
- The bridge (the plastic between the two finger holes) should be
  about 1/2 inch, as FitSlice's default. A 1/4 inch bridge is only right
  when the finger holes were cut at 31/32 inch for inserts; with
  finger-sized holes it leaves a hairline web deep inside and the app
  warns.
- The model warns you in the status line (and below the preview) when
  holes would run into each other, the web between the finger holes gets
  thin, or a bore would break through the base. The same warnings are
  repeated on the job sheet. Fix them before printing.
- Very large or odd values (spans over about 6 inches, hole sizes
  outside the normal range) are refused with a message instead of being
  built.
- The training parts are for swing-and-hold and carpet-release drills
  only. Never throw them.

Questions or problems: write down what you clicked and what the screen
said, and keep the job_sheet.txt for the order.
